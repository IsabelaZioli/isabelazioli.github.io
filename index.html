QA & Localization Case Studies

Coral Island — PT-BR Analysis

Two reports produced as an active player and designer, documenting bugs, localization failures, and UX improvement opportunities.

QA Report · Part 1

PT-BR Localization & QA Review

Analysis of grammar errors, wrong translations, untranslated dialogue, and a variable rendering bug that exposes internal code to the player.

Localization Technical Bug Narrative UX

4 documented issues with real gameplay screenshots, bug type, expected behavior, and player impact.

Findings breakdown

All screenshots were captured directly from my monitor during real gameplay sessions — unedited, to ensure authenticity.

Grammar bug Wrong verb form — "Não Dica" ▶

Message title with incorrect verb conjugation and unnatural capitalization, making the text feel unpolished in PT-BR.

Original: "Não Dica 'Eca' pra Comida dos Outros"
Suggested: "Não diga 'eca' para a comida dos outros"
Wrong translation "Negando" should be "nevando" (snowing) ▶

A single letter changes the entire meaning of the scene — "negando" (denying) has no connection to the snow context of the scene.

Original: "Tá negando lá fora"
Suggested: "Tá nevando lá fora"
Untranslated Emotional dialogue line left in English ▶

NPC dialogue line in English while player responses are in Portuguese — breaks immersion at a key emotional moment.

Original: "Come here, Isabela... I need a hug."
Suggested: "Vem aqui, Isabela... tô precisando de um abraço."
Variable bug <PlayerName> tag visible in dialogue text ▶

Player name variable is not rendered, exposing internal code to the player. The same pattern occurs with <portrait=happy> in another scene.

Displayed: "O que você acha, <PlayerName>Isabela?"
Expected: "O que você acha, Isabela?"
QA Report · Part 2

QA & UX Analysis — Cutscenes & NPCs

Deep dive into cutscene issues, NPC interactions, and UI inconsistencies — including contextual localization bugs and narrative logic failures.

Cutscenes NPC Dialogue Contextual Logic

5 documented issues — from the Left/Right inconsistency to Raina speaking English outside the Recreation Center.

Findings breakdown

All screenshots were captured directly from my monitor during real gameplay sessions — unedited, to ensure authenticity.

Technical bug <portrait=happy> tag visible in Eva's dialogue ▶

During a cutscene with Eva, the portrait control tag appears as raw text instead of being parsed by the system, exposing internal implementation to the player.

Localization "Left" untranslated in the walking cutscene ▶

In the scene teaching sea characters to walk, "Right" is correctly localized to PT-BR but "Left" appears in English — an inconsistency that breaks the guided narrative moment.

Grammar Missing verb in Aaliyah's dialogue ▶

Dialogue line with grammatically incorrect construction in PT-BR.

Original: "Deve ter legal ter casa própria."
Suggested: "Deve ser legal ter uma casa própria."
UI / Item Item name in English, description in PT-BR ▶

"Ruby red dragonet" keeps its English name while the description is in Portuguese, creating inconsistency in the inventory UI.

Contextual bug Raina speaks English outside the Recreation Center ▶

Raina's dialogue appears in English even with the game set to PT-BR, and references the Recreation Center during interactions that happen elsewhere — likely a contextual dialogue trigger issue.

Game Design Project

Island Rescue

A game concept developed during my degree — a farming game with ecological balance mechanics, where every player action carries an environmental responsibility.

🏝️

The concept

A character shipwrecks on a polluted island and faces a dilemma: fix the boat and leave, or stay and restore the ecosystem. He stays — but nothing in nature's balance comes for free.

🌱

Balance mechanic

Every tree cut requires replanting. No action disrupts the ecosystem without consequence.

🐟

Responsible fishing

Fishing is only allowed where a species is overpopulated. Diving requires cleaning trash and oil from the sea first.

🦕

Animals as allies

Rescue and raise cows, rabbits, dinosaurs, and ostriches — but they must accept you first.

🌾

Circular economy

Farm and harvest to eat, sell, or trade services with the island's mystical creatures.

✨

Purification quests

Magic potions to heal animals and special locations — earned through narrative tasks.

🎮

Aesthetic direction

3D visuals and gameplay inspired by Coral Island and Stardew Valley — fluid and accessible.
